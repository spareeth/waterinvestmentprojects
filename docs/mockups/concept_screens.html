01 · COUNTRY CONTROL

Countries own their information

Country focal points upload their own project teasers, confirm they can be shared, and decide which meeting requests to accept.

02 · INVITATION ONLY

Personal, verified access

Investors and operators are invited by the Summit team. Every account is personal, protected by two-step sign-in, and logged.

03 · FROM TEASER TO MEETING

Request a bilateral

Review the pipeline, express interest in specific projects and request a meeting slot. Countries review and confirm.
