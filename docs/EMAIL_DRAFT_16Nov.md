As promised, please find below the proposed outline of the project portal, a suggested name and web address, and a few points we need to confirm before countries start uploading.

**1. What we understood**

1. For the Summit, each project is represented by its one-page teaser in the World Bank Group template. We expect 25 to a maximum of 30 teasers from 9 countries, and no financial models.
2. Country focal points upload their own teasers and, before publishing, confirm through a checkbox that the teaser contains no confidential information and can be shared with invited participants.
3. Investors and operators are invited by email from a list curated by the Summit team. Once signed in, they can see all published teasers.
4. Investors can request a bilateral meeting or more information on a specific project. The country reviews the request and accepts or declines it.
5. If the venue has no other booking system for the two bilateral rooms, accepted meetings can be given 30-minute slots directly in the portal.
6. Beyond the Summit, GCFC sees the platform continuing as a fuller data room and pipeline discovery space. We propose to scope that separately once the Summit version is running.

**2. Proposed approach**

The portal will be a custom, invitation-only web platform developed by Expo City Dubai, through Terra. For the Summit phase, we propose to host it on a secure cloud server in Germany (EU) managed by Expo City Dubai; hosting for any longer-term platform can be agreed separately. Every account is personal, with two-step verification. Teasers are watermarked with the viewer's name, downloads can be switched off per teaser, and all access, acknowledgements and requests are logged. The attached screenshots are an early concept with fictional content, showing the landing page, the project pipeline, a teaser page with the meeting request, the country upload with the acknowledgement, and the country's request inbox with the optional slot booking.
